# Disclaimer
**Student learning project — please read.**

This chatbot is **not meant for commercial, professional, or legal use.** It is a student's learning project to explore Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and techniques for reducing AI hallucinations.

**No response should be considered 100% true.** Hallucinations can happen. Always verify against the official text of the Constitution of India (Legislative Department, Government of India) and consult a qualified lawyer for real legal matters.

Answers are generated only from a limited set of uploaded documents and may be incomplete, outdated (amendments), or wrong.

**Privacy:** no accounts, no database, no analytics. Questions are sent to the server only to answer them and are not stored. They are forwarded to Groq (language model) and, if enabled, Hugging Face (query embedding).
